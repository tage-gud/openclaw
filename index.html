What is OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is a powerful, open-source framework designed to facilitate the creation, deployment, and management of AI agents. It acts as a comprehensive workspace and runtime environment, providing the necessary tools and abstractions to build sophisticated AI agents capable of interacting with the digital and physical world. Think of it as an "operating system" for your AI, offering structure, persistent memory, tool integration, and lifecycle management
